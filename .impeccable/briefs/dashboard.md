# Today's run

Mode: operate. The owner opens this to see what goes out next and what failed.

Audience: the owner, at a desk or on a phone, checking Furnio and Smart Leads.
Task: read the next slot, retry a failed simulation, or start a post.
Content: sample workspace posts until the owner replaces them. Every result from the simulator says so.
Constraints: no ads, no live network calls, keyboard and touch, English.
